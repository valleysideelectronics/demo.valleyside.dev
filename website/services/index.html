Winterizing & de-winterizing

Protect your engine, plumbing and fuel from a hard Kentucky freeze, then wake everything up in spring for the first warm Saturday.

One cracked block costs more than a decade of winterizing. We stabilize and fog, flush antifreeze through the engine and plumbing, change the gear lube, and hook up a battery tender. In spring we reverse it all and test-run the boat before you pick it up.

What's included

  • Fuel stabilizer and engine fogging
  • Engine and cooling system antifreeze
  • Lower unit gear lube change
  • Plumbing, livewells and heads
  • Battery service and tender hookup
  • Spring start-up and test run

FAQ

Questions about service

Do you work on my brand of engine?

Yes, almost certainly. Our technicians are factory-trained on today's major outboard and sterndrive lines and have worked on just about every engine that's been on this lake since 1998. If a job needs a specialist, we'll tell you up front.

Are these prices final?

They're starting prices for typical boats. Your written estimate depends on engine size, boat length and condition. Parts are billed at the price on the estimate, and labor is $115 an hour.

How long will my boat be in the shop?

Typical turnaround is listed with each service. In spring and early summer things get busy, so we give you a realistic date when you drop off and text you if anything changes.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Our workmanship is covered for 90 days, and new parts carry their manufacturer's warranty. If something we fixed isn't right, bring it back and we'll make it right.

Can you pick up my boat?

Yes. We offer pickup and delivery within our service area starting at $95 each way, and it's included free with a full season of storage.

What forms of payment do you take?

All major cards, checks and cash. For storage customers we can split the season into monthly payments.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us what's going on and we'll point you in the right direction. No charge for advice over the phone.
