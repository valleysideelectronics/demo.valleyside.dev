Questions

Frequently asked

Do you work on my brand of engine?

Yes, almost certainly. Our technicians are factory-trained on today's major outboard and sterndrive lines and have worked on just about every engine that's been on this lake since 1998. If a job needs a specialist, we'll tell you up front.

How far does mobile service travel?

Our service van covers roughly 25 miles around the shop, including Millbrook Landing, Cedar Hollow, Otter Creek, Laurel Point, Pine Knob, Sawmill Bend, Stillwater and Hickory Flat. Farther than that? Call us and we'll see what we can do.

When should I book winterizing?

As early as you can. Most of our winterizing slots for late October and November are booked by mid-October. Booking early also locks in a spot for indoor storage.

Do you give estimates before starting work?

Always. You get a written estimate before we turn a wrench, and we call for your OK if we find anything else. No surprises on the invoice.
