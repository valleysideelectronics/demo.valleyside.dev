Our story

Two generations keeping Cedar Hollow Lake on the water

Bluestone Cove Marine started in a two-bay pole barn in 1998. It's still the same family, the same cove and the same phone number.

Since 1998

Built on word of mouth

Ray spent his twenties working on commercial boats and his evenings fixing outboards for neighbors. In 1998 he and Carla put up a pole barn above the cove, hung a hand-painted sign, and hoped for enough work to get through the first winter.

They got it, mostly because Ray would explain exactly what was wrong and Carla would call before anything cost more than the estimate. Twenty-eight seasons later, that is still how the shop runs.

Today their son Josh leads the service bays, Mia runs detailing, and Luis keeps the service van moving around the lake. Biscuit handles public relations.

Milestones

28 seasons on the cove

A lot has changed on the lake since 1998. The way we treat customers hasn't.

  1. 1998

    Two bays and a borrowed tractor

    Ray and Carla open the shop in a pole barn above the cove, fixing neighbors' outboards on nights and weekends.

  2. 2004

    Winterizing becomes its own season

    After a hard freeze cracked engines all over the lake, we added shrink-wrap and outdoor storage.

  3. 2011

    The indoor storage building

    A 40-boat indoor building goes up next to the shop, half of it heated.

  4. 2016

    The service van hits the road

    Mobile dockside service starts, for all the boats that live on lifts and never leave the water.

  5. 2022

    The second generation

    Josh takes over as lead technician, and the shop adds computer diagnostics for late-model engines.

  6. Today

    28 seasons, still family-run

    Five people, one shop dog, and more than 4,000 boats serviced. Same phone number since day one.
