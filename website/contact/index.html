    Before you ask

    Quick answers

    How fast will I hear back?

    During business hours we reply to quote requests within a couple of hours, usually with a phone call. Requests sent after hours get a reply the next business morning.

    Can I just drop my boat off?

    You can, but please call or send a request first so we can plan a bay for it. Drop-offs outside business hours can use the marked spots by the gate.

    How far does mobile service travel?

    Our service van covers roughly 25 miles around the shop, including Millbrook Landing, Cedar Hollow, Otter Creek, Laurel Point, Pine Knob, Sawmill Bend, Stillwater and Hickory Flat. Farther than that? Call us and we'll see what we can do.
